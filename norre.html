STRATEGY 01 / EDITORIAL POISE

Restraint Over Hype

Eliminating cheap promotional banners, "swipe-up" stickers, and loud urgency badges. Instead, utilizing high-end editorial crops, generous borders, and quiet Swiss typography that treats the audience as discerning connoisseurs.

STRATEGY 02 / 4:5 VERTICAL DISCIPLINE

Viewport Dominance

Every carousel and static post balances high-level silhouette art direction with extreme macro fiber details (680gsm British lambswool, horn hardware, hand-stitched bar tacks) to justify premium £420–£890 price points.
