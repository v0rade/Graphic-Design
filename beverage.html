Eliminating fake 3D studio reflections and neon rim-lighting. Each key visual is lit with single-source directional daylight (Mediterranean noon, sunset golden hour, and diffused north light) with real cast shadows.

Rejecting screaming consumer-packaged-goods (CPG) slogans and discount bursts. Headlines use confident editorial brevity (*"Bite Into Noon", "The 6:00 PM Pause"*) paired with unambiguous retail calls to action.

REJECTING SYNTHETIC 3D GLOSS

SCENARIO 01 // NOON DIRECTIONAL

Hard Sunlight & Plinth Shadow

Employs a single 1200W HMI spotlight positioned at 60 degrees overhead to simulate harsh Mediterranean summer sun. Hard cast shadows anchor the can to the textured sandstone without muddy artificial ambient fill.

COLOR TEMP: 5600K // HARD SHADOW CONTRAST
SCENARIO 02 // GOLDEN HOUR RIM

2800K Low-Angle Amber Glow

A low-slung tungsten fresnel cuts across the coupe glass from the rear three-quarter angle. Highlights the liquid meniscus, illuminates the clear ice sphere, and produces a gentle amber rim on the matte can edge.

COLOR TEMP: 2800K // BACKLIT TRANSMISSION
SCENARIO 03 // OVERHEAD SOFTBOX

Diffused Northern Skylight

A 150cm octagonal softbox directly overhead creates creamy, wrap-around light with zero harsh hotspots. Ideal for showcasing ingredient provenance, nutrition data blocks, and delicate botanical leaves.

COLOR TEMP: 4800K // HIGH DYNAMIC RANGE
