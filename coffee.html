01 / BRAND BRIEF

Elevating the bag into an archival artifact.

Specialty coffee packaging is heavily crowded with loud neon sticker collages, cartoon mascots, or ultra-glossy plastic pouches that fight for attention on retail shelves. MORA was built on a philosophy of architectural quietness: natural tactile kraft paper, disciplined typography, and deep traceability that honors the agricultural producers.

PILLAR 01

Origin Transparency

No proprietary blends or generic tasting notes. Elevation, processing method, washing station, and cupping scores are documented on the face with laboratory precision.

PILLAR 02

Tactile Sensation

Eliminating artificial foil sheen. The combination of unbleached Swedish kraft and tipped-on fibrous cotton label invites touch and feels authentic on kitchen counters.

PILLAR 03

System Scalability

A standardized base pouch paired with an interchangeable modular label allows MORA to launch hyper-limited micro-lot releases (under 50 bags) without re-tooling structural packaging.

ENGINEERING SPECIFICATIONS

Modular Labeling Architecture

By decoupling the base stand-up pouch (neutral kraft with degassing valve) from the information-bearing label, the roastery eliminates the minimum order quantity (MOQ) barrier of custom-printed foil pouches.

01 / SUBSTRATE MATERIAL

140gsm FSC-certified uncoated cotton paper. High tensile tear strength with water-resistant soy-oil overprint varnish.

02 / COLOR CODING SYSTEM

A 4mm top bleed bar instantly communicates the processing category: Sage (Washed), Terracotta (Anaerobic), and Umber (Honey).

03 / VARIABLE THERMAL ROAST WINDOW

The lower rectangle accommodates on-demand thermal imprint for exact roast date, batch lot, and roaster initials at packing time.

05 / SUSTAINABILITY & BARRIER SCIENCE

100% Home-Compostable Pouch Spec

ZERO ALUMINUM FOIL LINING // EN 13432 CERTIFIED

PLA / Bio-PBS Inner Barrier

Replaces conventional petrochemical aluminum foils with a plant-derived cornstarch PLA barrier film. Guarantees less than 0.5 cm³/m²/day oxygen transmission rate while breaking down in domestic compost within 90 days.

Compostable Degassing Valve

Molded from bio-based polybutylene succinate (Bio-PBS). Relieves carbon dioxide pressure emitted by freshly roasted coffee while sealing out ambient moisture and oxygen, extending freshness for 12 weeks.

Water-Soluble Soy Inks

All text and color codes are printed with heavy-metal-free vegetable soy oil inks. The label cleanly separates during paper repulping processes with zero toxic chemical runoff.
