12-Column Brutalist Grid

Employs a dense 12-column Swiss system with asymmetric 7:5 proportional balance. High-density text bands run strictly on a 4pt baseline grid to maintain optical alignment across multi-tier performer listings.

SPOT SILKSCREEN INK

Fluorescent Vermilion

Formulated using day-glo pigment with high UV-reflectance. Screen-printed via 120T mesh over a dense opaque white underbase for extreme chromatic brilliance under low gallery lighting.

SCULPTURAL EMBOSSING

Blind Multi-Level Die

CNC-milled brass die pressed into 300gsm G.F Smith Munken board under 40 tons of pressure, creating a permanent 1.2mm tactile depression with zero ink or discoloration.

HOT FOIL STAMPING

Matte Platinum Silver

Kurz Luxor matte metallic foil stamped at 130°C onto dark oceanic board. Produces a non-glossy, mercury-like liquid sheen that catches ambient directional gallery lighting.
